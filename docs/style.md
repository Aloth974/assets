# Art direction

Sanitized copy of Apolarion's internal art direction, synced from private revision
`ba32d3c`. Every asset submitted here is judged against it.

## The direction

**Stylised and hand-painted, matching World of Warcraft's art.** World of Warcraft is the
reference for the whole look: distance read, an inviting and inhabited world. Rayman is the
reference for confident painted materials. The anti-references are Fallout and Baldur's
Gate: realistic, gritty, desaturated.

**Quality is the quality of the stylisation and of its light.** Detail serves the form and
never competes with it. Forms are few, smooth and rounded; nothing is blocky, faceted or
clunky. Imperfection is coherent: nothing is quite straight, and nothing is noise.

**The first read is welcome; the second is mystery.** Friendly is not comic; eerie is not
dark, muddy or hostile.

**The reference look:** chunky, oversized forms (thick beams, broad doors, every stone its
own rounded block); dense painted texture in large strokes; complementary colour pairs and
no pure black; a low warm sun and soft bounce; warm haze over the far distance; a sky of
pink and peach painted cloud masses with mauve shadows; broad-bladed grass lit as one mass.

## World grammar

- **Invitation before spectacle.** Safe places, paths and useful things are broad, open and
  readable by shape. Danger may be sharp, but keeps a route around it.
- **The impossible is precise.** Small, sourced magical details give ordinary things their
  strangeness. Never repeat one glowing accent everywhere.
- **Every surface reads as its material** (stone, wood, plaster), never as flat colour.
- **A wood reads as a forest**: large trees, clustered, never an even stipple.

## Silhouette

- **One dominant feature per asset**, exaggerated past realism: a tree's canopy many times
  its trunk.
- **Recognisable as a flat black shape.** If it needs its texture, the shape is wrong.
- **Proportions are stylised and exaggerated**, never realistic anatomy.
- **Buildings have exaggerated, never rigid profiles**: flared eaves, overhangs, tapered
  towers.

## Scale

Measured against a 1.80 m human, architecture is exaggerated, furniture a little, and what
a hand holds not at all:

| Element | × the human | Metres |
|---|---|---|
| Door head | 2.0 | 3.6 |
| Storey, homes and shops | 2.5 | 4.5 |
| Storey, public buildings | ≥ 3 | ≥ 5.4 |
| Furniture, dressing, lamps | 1.2 | a table 1.0 |
| Held items, characters | 1.0 | — |

## Colour and texture

- **Painted, not photographic.** A surface is a few large painted value regions with
  deliberate edges. Painted wear and scratches belong; photographic microdetail and scanned
  or photoreal textures do not.
- **Detail is suggested, never spelled out.** The eye fills in the rest.
- **The texture carries the light**: painted shadow, bounce and rim stay in it.
- **What matters to play stands out** from its background.

## Light and glow

- Low warm sun, atmospheric depth, soft grade. Ground and sky always separate.
- **Glow has a source**: crystals, essence, magma, forges, embers, fires. Nothing else
  glows.

## Icons

An icon is one subject, at most one modifier and a quiet background.

- **First read:** one dominant silhouette names the item, action or state at 24–32 px.
  Material and tier are second reads.
- **Second read:** one direction, target or state cue. Texture and wear reward a larger
  view but never carry the identity. Hue is never the only meaning.
- **Light and material:** one upper-left key light and cool violet bounce. Steel, cloth,
  wood, stone, glass, ice and essence keep distinct value and surface behaviour. Warm gold
  is not a mandatory rim on cold materials.
- **Family grammar:** weapons share one diagonal, pommel low-left, working end dominant;
  consumables and resources show the object before its effect; abilities show the actual
  action; buffs enclose or rise, debuffs interrupt, bind, crack or fall; interface marks
  use fewer details than items.
- **Gear views:** helm front with face opening; pauldrons as a pair on a faint bust; chest
  with neckline and arm holes; one glove from the back with four fingers, thumb and cuff;
  boot in profile, toe right; hooded cloak; belt closed around its buckle.

## Anti-goals

Photographic or scanned texture; realistic anatomy; the blocky, faceted or clunky; the
ordinary made vaguely magical; anything that reads as Fallout or Baldur's Gate.
